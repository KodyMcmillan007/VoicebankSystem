Crypton Future Media

The creators of the first functional voicebanks known for public selling,

Crypton was the stepping stones for all of the following voicebanks; with voicebanks like Kaito and Mekio being the first two major voicebanks to be created by Crypton. Crypton itself was founded in July 1995.


Twindrill

The fan-created management team behind the Utauloid known as Kasane Teto,

Twindrill holds the primary copyright and ownership rights for Kasnae Teto, acting on behalf of her original illustrator (Sen) and the voice provider (Mayo Oyamno)

Twindrill as establised in 2009-following Teto's viral debut as an April fool's day 'Fake voceloid' joke on the Japanese textboard 2channel in 2008 to manage her intellectual property and support fan creative activites.


Internet Co. Ltd.

A Japanese company based in Osaka that specializes in music production, audio editing tools, and singing synthesizers. It was Founded in september 1988.

They ae best known early on for the music sequencer 'singer song writer' and the modern digital audio workstation (DAW) ABILITY.


Dreamtonics Co., Ltd.

A Tokyo-based music technology company specializing in vocal production, artifical intelligence singing voice synthesis, and speech technology.

Founded by Kanru Hua, the company is best known for creating Synthesizer V studio , a flagflish virtual session singer software that delivers highly realistic mutlingual vocal synthesis though deep neural network technology.
